About Us

Our Story

CRAFTED BY WAV started from a childhood hobby of creating invitations, designs, and crafts. During the pandemic, we started printing our designs and turning them into different products. The name CRAFTED BY WAV comes from the three siblings—Aple, Winmer, and Vea. Today, we offer affordable and customized printing products for school, personal use, businesses, and special occasions.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide high-quality, affordable, and creative printing services while helping bring every customer's ideas to life, especially for students and customers on a budget.
